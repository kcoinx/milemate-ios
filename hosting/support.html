Support

Need help with mileage tracking, your account, purchases, reports, or data deletion?

Email MileMate Support

Support email: kcoinx@gmail.com

What to include

  • A short description of the problem or request.
  • Your MileMate app version and build number from Settings → About MileMate.
  • Your iOS version and the steps that led to the issue.

Do not email passwords, Apple or Google credentials, payment-card details, private keys, or sensitive trip coordinates.

Account and local-data deletion

If you can open MileMate, use Settings → Account → Delete Account to delete your authenticated account and its locally owned MileMate data. Guests can use Delete All MileMate Data in Settings.

If you cannot access the app, email support and explain that you need account or data-deletion assistance. Identity verification may be required before an authenticated account can be changed.

Feedback and support records

Feedback and support submissions are assigned a 365-day retention period from receipt. After that, they become eligible for automated Firestore TTL deletion. Removal may occur after the expiration date and is not immediate.

Deleting a MileMate account does not automatically delete a previously submitted feedback or support message. To request earlier removal, email support with the approximate submission date, submission type or category, any reply email you included, and a short non-sensitive phrase from the message. If the record cannot be matched reliably, MileMate will not risk deleting another person's submission.

Subscriptions

Deleting MileMate or deleting an account does not cancel App Store billing. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.
